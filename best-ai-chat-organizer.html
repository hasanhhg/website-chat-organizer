The Best AI Chat Organizers for ChatGPT and Claude

There are dozens of Chrome extensions that promise to tidy up your ChatGPT and Claude sidebar. They mostly work in one of two ways, and that choice matters more than any feature list. Here are seven options, compared side by side.

Quick answer

Want your existing chats sorted for you? Use Chat Organizer. It files them into ChatGPT's and Claude's own Projects after you approve a preview, and it is free.

Want folders, full-text search and export across four AIs? Look at AI Toolbox or Sortbase, and expect to pay for the full feature set.

Only use Claude and want simple manual folders? Folders for Claude is free.

We make Chat Organizer, so weigh our pick with that in mind. Everything said here about the other tools comes from their own Chrome Web Store listing or pricing page, checked on 12 November 2026. Prices and features change often, so check before you install.

Two kinds of chat organizer

Auto-sorters read your chat titles, work out what each chat is about, and move it into a group for you. You review the result instead of doing the filing. This suits people with a backlog of hundreds of chats who will never sort them by hand.

Folder extensions add a folder panel to the sidebar. You create folders and drag chats into them yourself. They usually come with extras like search, export, bulk delete and a prompt library. This suits people who want precise control and are happy to file chats one by one.

There is a second difference that is easy to miss: where your order is stored. Folder extensions keep their folders inside the extension, in your browser or in an account with that extension. Those folders do not show up in the ChatGPT or Claude app on your phone, and they depend on the extension staying installed. ChatGPT and Claude both have their own Projects feature. A chat moved into a Project stays there everywhere you open ChatGPT or Claude, with or without any extension.

The options, one by one

1. Chat Organizer

ChatGPT and Claude · auto-sort into Projects · free

Chat Organizer is the one we build. Click Organize and it reads up to your 200 most recent chats, matches each unorganized title against your Project names with a multilingual keyword list, and asks an AI only about titles it cannot place. You see a grouped preview of every move, then click Apply and the chats land in real Projects. It needs no API key and no account.

Good for: a messy backlog you want sorted in one go, and order that also shows up in the ChatGPT and Claude apps on your phone.

Watch out: it does not do full-text search, export or a prompt library. Each run only looks at your 200 most recent chats. Titles it cannot place by keyword are sent to a shared AI service; chat content never is.

2. AI Toolbox (formerly ChatGPT Toolbox)

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok · folders plus many extras · free plan, paid plans

The broadest toolkit in this list. It adds nested folders, including folders that mix chats from all four AIs, full-text search through message content, bulk export and delete, a prompt library with prompt chains, and automatic topic tags. ChatGPT gets every feature; Claude, Gemini and Grok share a common set. Your folder structure syncs to an AI Toolbox account.

Good for: heavy users who want one toolkit for everything and will use the extras.

Watch out: filing chats into folders is still up to you, and the full feature set is paid.

3. Sortbase (formerly Easy Folders)

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok · cross-platform folders · paid after trial

Sortbase turns your chats from four AIs into one workspace with folders, search and a prompt library. It is one of the most used tools here, with about 10,000 users and a 4.1 rating from 421 reviews on the Chrome Web Store.

Good for: people who switch between several AIs every day and want one place for all of it.

Watch out: premium features cost $10 a month or $100 a year after a 7-day trial, and the trial asks for a card.

4. Superpower for ChatGPT

ChatGPT only · folders, search, prompts, export · free tier, paid Pro

One of the longest-running ChatGPT extensions, still updated in November 2026. It adds folders, search, prompt management, notes and export on top of chatgpt.com.

Good for: people who live in ChatGPT and want many small upgrades in one install.

Watch out: it covers ChatGPT only, and you file chats yourself.

5. Folders for Claude

Claude only · manual folders · free

A simple, free folder panel for the claude.ai sidebar with subfolders and drag and drop. It searches all chat titles and keeps an archive of every chat it has seen, which helps you find chats that dropped off Claude's recent list. Data stays in your browser unless you switch on its optional encrypted sync.

Good for: Claude-only users who want manual folders without a subscription.

Watch out: no ChatGPT support, and the folders exist only inside the extension.

6. AI Chat Folders

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini · manual folders · free up to 10 folders

A newer, lightweight folder tool for three AIs with drag and drop, bulk delete and title search. The free version allows 10 folders; Pro costs $8 a month for unlimited folders and export.

Good for: trying out folders across several AIs without paying up front.

Watch out: it is young (version 0.3 at the time of writing), and several Pro features were still listed as coming soon.

7. Built-in Projects, no extension

ChatGPT and Claude · manual · included

Both ChatGPT and Claude let you create Projects and move chats into them from each chat's menu. It costs nothing and works everywhere, including the mobile apps.

Good for: a few dozen chats, or keeping new chats tidy from day one.

Watch out: at roughly ten seconds per chat, a backlog of 300 chats is close to an hour of clicking. Step-by-step instructions are in our ChatGPT guide and Claude guide.

How to choose

  • You have hundreds of unsorted chats and no time: pick an auto-sorter. Filing by hand is the step most people never finish.
  • You use ChatGPT or Claude on your phone too: prefer anything that uses the platform's own Projects, because extension folders stay in the desktop browser.
  • You need to search inside old conversations or export them: a toolkit such as AI Toolbox or Sortbase does that; an organizer alone does not.
  • You use Gemini or Grok as well: AI Toolbox, Sortbase and AI Chat Folders cover more than ChatGPT and Claude.
  • You do not want another subscription: Chat Organizer, Folders for Claude and the built-in Projects cost nothing.

The two approaches also combine well. You can let Chat Organizer sort your backlog into Projects once, then use a folder or search tool for the extras you need.
